AULA S35 PRO driver, without the download

This page replaces the AULA S35 PRO Windows software (the official installer is named “AULA-S350PRO”). It talks to the mouse directly from your browser (WebHID), so there's nothing to install, and every setting is saved on the mouse itself.

  • DPI – up to 8 stages from 100 to 12000 DPI, each with its own colour.
  • Polling rate – 125, 250, 500 or 1000 Hz, plus debounce time.
  • Buttons – remap all 6 buttons and the scroll wheel: keys, key combos, media keys, DPI and profile switching, double click, rapid fire.
  • Macros – record keystrokes, clicks and scrolls with timing.
  • RGB lighting – all 9 effects with brightness, speed, direction and colours.
  • Power and sensor – sleep timers, wake on movement, angle snapping, ripple control and motion smoothing.
  • 3 profiles – Office, Game I and Game II, with backup and restore.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download the AULA S35 PRO driver?

You don't need to. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera, plug in the mouse (USB cable or 2.4G receiver), click Connect mouse and choose “2.4G Gaming Mouse” or “USB Gaming Mouse”.

Which browsers and operating systems work?

Any desktop browser with WebHID: Chrome, Edge or Opera on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS. Firefox and Safari don't support it.

The mouse doesn't show up or won't connect.

Close the official AULA software first. On the 2.4G receiver, click a mouse button to wake it, then connect again. On Linux you may need a udev rule that gives your user access to hidraw devices.

Do my settings stay after I close the page?

Yes. Changes are written to the mouse's memory, so they work on any computer without this page.

Is this official AULA software?

No. It's an independent, open-source tool that uses the same protocol as the official Windows driver. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AULA.
